Our Story

founded in 2018, Habesha Eatery brings the authentic test of Ethiopia to Addis Ababa. Our journey began with a simple mission: to share the rich culinary heritage of the Horn of Africa with the world.

Every dish tells a story of traditional, family, and community. For our signature Doro Wate to our vvegetarian platters, we use only the freshset ingredients and time-honored recipes.

Located in the vibrant Bole District, We've become a gathering place for locals and visitors alike to experiance the warmth of Ethiopian hospitality.
